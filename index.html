VALUATION

Price paid vs history

Current P/E is compared with each segment's own 5-year median. The historical anchor is slow-moving and can safely use the last verified value if its source is temporarily unavailable.

PROFIT PULSE

What companies are reporting now

Market-cap-weighted latest-quarter YoY profit growth is winsorized to reduce tiny-base distortions. Profit breadth shows how much of the segment is participating.

BREADTH

Participation under the index

% of covered constituents above their 50-day and 200-day moving averages. Breadth is optional: if Yahoo is unavailable, the core dashboard still refreshes.

QUALITY

Earnings yield & ROE proxy

Implied ROE proxy = P/B ÷ P/E. Useful for broad comparison, not a substitute for company-level ROE analysis.

RELATIVE VALUE

Premium vs Large Cap

Current Mid/Small P/E premium is compared with the premium implied by each segment's own 5-year median P/E. This avoids forecasting a future multiple.

RISK

Volatility, beta & drawdown

Volatility and beta are slow-moving risk anchors; drawdown from the 52-week high comes from the live index page.

OPPORTUNITY

Opportunity score /100

Higher = more supportive observable evidence across valuation, current profits, breadth, quality and momentum.

RISK

METHODOLOGY V3.0

How the framework works

Opportunity score

Risk score

Architecture: v3 deliberately uses ordinary public HTML pages, not undocumented/private chart APIs. Screener is the primary live source; IndexPE supplies slow-moving historical/risk anchors; Yahoo is optional for constituent breadth.

Valuation: current live P/E versus each segment's own 5-year median. If IndexPE is temporarily unavailable, the last verified median is retained rather than blocking the whole dashboard.

Relative valuation: Mid/Small current P/E premium versus Nifty 50 compared with the premium implied by their respective 5-year median P/Es.

Profit pulse: market-cap-weighted latest-quarter YoY constituent profit growth plus the share of covered market cap with positive profit growth. Extreme growth rates are capped before weighting to reduce base effects.

Breadth: constituent participation above 50DMA and 200DMA. Missing breadth does not stop valuation/profit/quality scores from refreshing; weights reallocate across available evidence.

Quality: index-level implied ROE proxy (P/B ÷ P/E).

Momentum: 1-year Mid/Small price performance relative to Large Cap. Large is the neutral benchmark.

Risk: valuation stretch, excess relative premium, realised volatility, beta, weak breadth and weak profit evidence are scored separately.

Source failures: a source failure is labelled live / partial / reference / cached in Data Health. The site preserves last-good values rather than silently replacing them with blanks.
