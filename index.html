METHODOLOGY

How the 10-point score works

Valuation /4

Current P/E compared with its own 5-year median.

  • >20% cheaper: 4
  • 10-20% cheaper: 3
  • Within +/-10%: 2
  • 10-20% expensive: 1
  • >20% expensive: 0

Earnings /4

Year-on-year growth in trailing index earnings, calculated as Index Level / P/E.

  • >15%: 4
  • 8-15%: 3
  • 0-8%: 2
  • -10% to 0%: 1
  • <-10%: 0

Momentum /2

Mid and Small are compared with Nifty 50 over 6 and 12 months.

  • Beats Large in both: 2
  • Beats Large in one: 1
  • Beats Large in neither: 0
  • Large benchmark: neutral 1
8-10 High research priority 6-7 Worth deeper research 4-5 Mixed setup 0-3 Demanding setup
