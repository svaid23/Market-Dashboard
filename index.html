Evidence framework, not buy/sell
VALUATION

Historical positioning

Percentile = where today's multiple sits within its own 5-year history. Lower is cheaper.

EARNINGS

Growth & acceleration

Acceleration compares current YoY trailing earnings growth with the YoY growth rate three months earlier.

BREADTH

Participation under the index

% of constituents above their 50-day and 200-day moving averages. Coverage is shown when data is partial.

QUALITY

Earnings yield & ROE proxy

Implied ROE proxy = P/B divided by P/E. It is useful at index level but is not a substitute for company-level ROE analysis.

RELATIVE VALUE

Premium vs Large Cap

The premium percentile shows whether today's Mid/Small P/E premium to Nifty 50 is low or high versus its own 5-year history.

RISK

Volatility & drawdown

Volatility is annualised from daily returns. Max drawdown is the largest peak-to-trough decline over the last year.

OPPORTUNITY

Opportunity score /100

Higher = more supportive observable evidence across valuation, earnings, breadth, quality and momentum.

RISK

Risk score /100

METHODOLOGY V2.4

How the framework works

Opportunity score

Risk score

Valuation: P/E percentile versus the segment's own 5-year history, with current P/B shown as a second valuation lens. Historical P/B percentile is intentionally omitted because the no-login source is not reliable enough for unattended automation.

Relative valuation: Mid/Small P/E premium versus Nifty 50, compared with that premium's own 5-year distribution.

Earnings: actual trailing index earnings growth plus whether that growth rate is accelerating or decelerating.

Breadth: constituent participation above 50DMA and 200DMA; a broad rally is treated differently from a narrow index move.

Quality: index-level implied ROE proxy (P/B ÷ P/E), converted to an absolute quality score rather than a historical percentile.

Momentum: Mid/Small 6M and 12M performance relative to Large Cap. Large is the neutral benchmark.

Risk: valuation stretch, relative premium, realised volatility, max drawdown, weak breadth and earnings deterioration are scored separately.

Missing data: scores automatically reweight across available components and show score coverage. Breadth can fall back to the last successful refresh.
